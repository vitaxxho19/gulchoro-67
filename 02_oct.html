Before You Go

  1. Choose a route.
    • Check distance and duration.
    • Read the preparation checklist.
  2. Prepare your equipment.
    • Water and food
    • Comfortable shoes
    • Weather-appropriate clothing
  3. Complete the application form.
What does "leave no trace" mean?

It means leaving nature as you found it: taking all trash with you, respecting wildlife, and staying on designated trails.
